# mindreply-org-site — Brand Surface

Public brand and acquisition surface for Mind-Reply.

Serious human-communication infrastructure presence — not a generic AI wrapper.

## Who is this for?

People and businesses evaluating Mind-Reply who need a clear, trustworthy public entry point.

## What human problem does it remove?

Confusion about what Mind-Reply actually is, who it serves, and what is verifiably live today.

## What is the smallest meaningful action?

Land on the site, understand the core promise in seconds, and take one clear next step (explore, contact, or activate).

## What happens when the system is wrong?

Only verified live capabilities are presented as live. Planned, beta, or private items are labelled honestly. No unsupported claims.
